These two images are meant to be used in the `CompareDemo.tsx` slider. They need to represent the exact same location/angle, but showing a raw construction site vs a finished building.

Generate these images in a **16:9 widescreen aspect ratio** (e.g., `--ar 16:9` in Midjourney). Convert them to **`.webp`** format and place them inside the **`public/assets/images/Banner`** folder.
